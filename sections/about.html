01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About Me

I’m Mohammad Jahed Murad Sunny, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock and a Graduate Research Assistant at the Emerging Analytics Center. I build interactive systems and study how people use them, combining software development, human–computer interaction, and data analysis.

I enjoy challenges that require me to learn something unfamiliar, work through uncertainty, and turn an idea into a working system. Whether I’m debugging a Unity application, integrating AI services, or analyzing movement data, I like understanding why something behaves the way it does and finding a practical way to improve it.

My work spans virtual reality, AI-enabled interaction, and behavioral analytics. I develop immersive applications with Unity, C#, and OpenXR, and use Python and machine learning to investigate motion precision, cognitive load, and user performance. My projects include real-time gaze analytics, telemetry replay, and voice-interactive agents that connect speech recognition, language models, and synthesized speech with virtual environments.

My background in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) gave me a foundation in hardware and systems thinking. Working in cellphone quality control at Walton Digi-Tech Industries Limited strengthened my experience in testing, troubleshooting, and product quality. I later completed my M.S. in Computer Science and a Graduate Certificate in Data Science at UA Little Rock.

I’m interested in opportunities where I can contribute to software engineering, applied AI, or XR research and development. I want to work on problems that demand careful engineering and experimentation, alongside people who challenge me to keep learning.

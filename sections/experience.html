Before becoming a Graduate Research Assistant at the Emerging Analytics Center (EAC) at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock (UALR), my professional path was defined by leadership, operational, and technical roles across both academia and industry. Each experience has shaped my expertise in management, engineering, and applied research.

University of Arkansas at Little Rock, (Aug 2023 – Dec 2025)

Supporting a graduate-level seminar course on Operating Systems. My responsibilities include facilitating in-class discussions, evaluating student-led presentations, and assisting with grading and feedback on seminar topics. This role allows me to engage with advanced OS concepts such as kernel architecture, scheduling algorithms, virtualization, and security, while fostering critical thinking and communication among graduate peers.

Graduate Research Assistant

Conducting interdisciplinary research in Virtual Reality (VR), immersive visualization, and machine learning. My work focuses on usability analytics, spatial decision-making, and gaze-based interaction design in XR. The EAC is a hub for innovation in immersive visualization, cybersecurity, IoT, and AI-driven applications, where I contribute to both technical and usability-focused research.
